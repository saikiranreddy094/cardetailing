About Elite Detailing

Driven by passion. Defined by perfection. Discover the standard of excellence in automotive care.

Our Story & Mission

Founded in 2016, Elite Detailing began with a singular focus: to elevate automotive care from a basic service into a specialized craft. We recognized a gap in the market for true, uncompromising paint correction and long-term ceramic protection.

Our mission is simple. We aim to preserve the automotive heritage and investments of our clients through meticulous attention to detail, cutting-edge chemical technology, and unmatched transparency.

The Elite Standard

Treating every vehicle as if it were on the concours lawn.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every machine pass and coating application.

Uncompromising Quality

Complete Transparency

Through our client portal, you see exactly what we see. No hidden defects, no exaggerated claims.

Continuous Education

The Environment

A State-of-the-Art Facility.

World-class results require a world-class environment. Our facility was purpose-built to execute flawless paint correction and optimal ceramic coating curing.

Climate Controlled

Maintained at precisely 72 degrees and 50% humidity for perfect coating flashes.

Scangrip Illumination

Secure & Monitored

Sustainable Detailing Practices

We believe that preserving your vehicle should not come at the cost of the environment. Elite Detailing is committed to eco-friendly practices across our entire operation.

Water Reclamation

Our advanced filtration system captures and purifies runoff, reducing our water consumption by 80% compared to traditional detailers.

VOC-Compliant

We utilize low-VOC and biodegradable compounds for interior cleaning, ensuring the air inside your cabin remains safe and pure.
