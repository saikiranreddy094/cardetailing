The Mastercraft Process

How we transform your vehicle from intake to delivery.

1. Deep Inspection

Ultrasonic paint depth readings and multi-spectrum light defect mapping.

2. Decontamination

Chemical iron-removal and mechanical clay bar treatment to purify the clear coat.

3. Paint Correction

Precision machine compounding to permanently level scratches and holograms.

4. Ceramic Lock

Application of 9H/10H matrices, followed by shortwave infrared curing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your appointment.

Depending on the package you choose, our coatings are warranted for 1 to 5 years. The longevity heavily depends on how the vehicle is maintained. We provide a comprehensive care guide and offer an annual maintenance wash program to ensure your coating reaches its maximum lifespan.

Ceramic coatings act as a magnifying glass. If we apply a 10H coating over swirl marks, scratches, or water spots, those defects will be permanently locked under the coating and highly visible. Paint correction levels the clear coat, ensuring a flawless, mirror-like finish before we seal it.
