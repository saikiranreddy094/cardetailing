Master Paint Correction

Flawless Surface Restoration

Paint correction is the artisanal process of leveling the clear coat to safely remove wash-induced swirls, scratches, water spots, and oxidation. It is the mandatory foundation before any ceramic coating application.

  • Single Stage: Enhances gloss and removes 50-60% of light defects.
  • Multi-Stage: Removes 85-95% of heavy defects for a mirror finish.
  • Wet Sanding: Specialized leveling for severe scratches and orange peel.
The Ultimate Protection

Nanoceramic Coatings

Traditional waxes last weeks; sealants last months. Our professional-grade 9H and 10H ceramic coatings bond molecularly with your factory clear coat to provide years of protection against UV rays, chemical stains, and bird droppings.

  • Extreme Hydrophobics: Water and dirt slide right off.
  • Candy-Like Gloss: Amplifies the depth and clarity of your paint.
  • Self-Cleaning Effect: Significantly reduces routine maintenance time.

Elite Maintenance Program

Exclusive to Coating Clients

To ensure your ceramic coating performs flawlessly for its entire lifespan, proper maintenance is required. We offer an exclusive, invitation-only maintenance wash program for vehicles we have coated to preserve your warranty and gloss.

  • PH-Neutral Hand Wash: Utilizing a strict two-bucket method to prevent micro-marring.
  • Annual Decontamination: Chemical removal of embedded iron and tar to restore hydrophobic properties.
  • Silica Spray Sealant: Applied every 6 months to rejuvenate the coating's sacrificial top layer.

The Science of Protection

Why we strictly apply Ceramic Coatings over traditional dealership waxes.

Traditional Wax / Sealant

  • Lasts only 4-8 weeks
  • Sits entirely on top of the clear coat
  • Melts at high summer temperatures (140°F+)
  • Zero resistance to harsh chemicals or bird droppings
The Elite Standard

Nanoceramic Coating

  • Guaranteed durability for 1 to 5+ years
  • Bonds at a molecular level with the factory paint
  • Withstands extreme heat and thermal shock (1200°F+)
  • High resistance to acids, sap, and environmental fallout
